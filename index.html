How to Play Pen Fight

STEP 1

Aim Your Shot

Click/touch and drag back from your pen. The cyan laser line shows your initial strike trajectory.

STEP 2

Charge Pullback Power

Pull back further to charge power (Low → Med → High → Max). Caution: 100% power can fly off the desk!

STEP 3

Choose Contact Point

Hit the Tip for sharp curve hooks, Center for straight ramming, or Tail for wide sweeps.

STEP 4

Score the Knockout!

Push your opponent's Center of Mass off the desk boundary. Win best 2 out of 3 rounds to become the Champion!
